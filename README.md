# GRC Risk Management Portfolio

This repository contains simulated Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) risk management projects designed to demonstrate practical cybersecurity risk management skills.

## Objectives

- Identify and document organizational risks
- Assess risk likelihood and impact
- Develop risk treatment strategies
- Create and maintain risk registers
- Produce audit-ready risk documentation
- Apply industry frameworks to risk management activities

## Skills Demonstrated

- Risk Identification
- Risk Assessment
- Risk Analysis
- Risk Treatment Planning
- Risk Register Development
- Risk Reporting
- Governance and Compliance

## Projects Included

### Project 1 – Risk Register Development
- Identify business and technology risks
- Assign likelihood and impact scores
- Determine risk ratings
- Recommend treatment plans
