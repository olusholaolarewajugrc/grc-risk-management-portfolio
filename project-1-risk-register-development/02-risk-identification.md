# Risk Identification

## Objective

The objective of this phase is to identify organizational risks that could negatively impact business operations, information assets, regulatory compliance, and service availability.

The identified risks were documented using the GRC Practice Lab and categorized according to their primary business or security domain.

---

# Risk Identification Methodology

The following approach was used:

1. Review the organizational risk register.
2. Identify threats affecting critical business processes.
3. Classify each risk into its appropriate category.
4. Record the business owner.
5. Capture the likelihood and impact scores.
6. Prioritize risks for further assessment and treatment.
