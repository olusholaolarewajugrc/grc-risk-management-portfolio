# Project 1 – Risk Register Development

## Objective

To identify, assess, document, and prioritize organizational risks through the creation of a structured risk register.

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates the risk management process used by organizations to identify threats, evaluate risk exposure, determine treatment strategies, and maintain risk visibility through a formal risk register.

The project follows a standard risk management lifecycle:

1. Risk Identification
2. Risk Assessment
3. Risk Prioritization
4. Risk Treatment Planning
5. Risk Monitoring

## Skills Demonstrated

* Risk Identification
* Risk Assessment
* Risk Analysis
* Likelihood and Impact Scoring
* Risk Prioritization
* Risk Treatment Planning
* Risk Documentation
* Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)

## Deliverables

### 01-risk-identification.md

Documents identified risks affecting the organization.

### 02-risk-assessment.md

Evaluates risks based on likelihood and impact.

### 03-risk-treatment-plan.md

Defines mitigation strategies, control recommendations, risk owners, and treatment decisions.

### evidence/

Contains supporting documentation and screenshots related to the risk register exercise.

## Expected Outcome

A documented risk register that provides visibility into organizational risks and supports risk-based decision-making.
