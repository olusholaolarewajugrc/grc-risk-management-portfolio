# Project 2 – Third-Party Vendor Risk Management

## Objective

To identify, assess, document, and monitor third-party vendor risks through a structured vendor risk management process.

---

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates the third-party risk management process used by organizations to identify critical vendors, evaluate vendor risks, determine appropriate treatment strategies, and continuously monitor vendor security and compliance.

The project follows a standard third-party risk management lifecycle:

1. Vendor Inventory
2. Vendor Classification
3. Vendor Risk Assessment
4. Vendor Risk Treatment
5. Ongoing Vendor Monitoring

---

## Skills Demonstrated

- Third-Party Risk Management (TPRM)
- Vendor Inventory Management
- Vendor Risk Assessment
- Vendor Criticality Classification
- Risk Scoring
- Risk Treatment Planning
- Vendor Monitoring
- Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC)

---

## Deliverables

### 01-vendor-inventory.md

Documents the organization's third-party vendors, services provided, business owners, and criticality classifications.

### 02-vendor-risk-assessment.md

Evaluates vendor security, compliance, financial, and operational risks using assessment scores.

### 03-vendor-risk-treatment.md

Documents recommended treatment actions for vendors based on assessment results and organizational risk tolerance.

### 04-vendor-risk-summary.md

Provides an executive summary of the organization's third-party vendor risk posture and recommended priorities.

### evidence/

Contains supporting screenshots captured from the GRC Practice Lab demonstrating vendor inventory and vendor risk assessment activities.

---

## Expected Outcome

A documented third-party vendor risk management process that demonstrates practical experience identifying, assessing, documenting, and managing vendor risks using a realistic GRC environment.
