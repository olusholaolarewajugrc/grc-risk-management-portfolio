# Vendor Risk Treatment

## Objective

The objective of this phase is to document the risk treatment information available for third-party vendor-related risk within the GRC Practice Lab.

The treatment information was reviewed from the Risk Treatments section of the GRC Practice Lab and documented based strictly on the treatment records displayed in the lab.

---

# Risk Treatment Methodology

The GRC Practice Lab provides treatment strategies for identified risks using the following treatment options:

- Mitigate
- Accept
- Transfer
- Avoid

Treatment records also identify the treatment status, responsible owner, target date, and treatment plan or description.

---

# Treatment Plan

The treatment record for **Vendor data breach affecting customers** contains the following plan and description:

1. Review cyber insurance.
2. Require SOC 2 from vendors.
3. Update contracts.

The treatment strategy recorded in the GRC Practice Lab is **Transfer**.

The treatment status is **Planned**, with **Legal** identified as the owner and **2026-07-14** recorded as the target date.

---

# Treatment Strategy

The treatment record identifies **Transfer** as the selected strategy for the vendor-related risk.

The GRC Practice Lab describes the treatment strategy as part of its Risk Treatments module. The specific treatment record documents the actions of reviewing cyber insurance, requiring SOC 2 from vendors, and updating contracts.

No additional vendor-specific treatment information is inferred beyond what is displayed in the GRC Practice Lab.

# Vendor Identification Limitation
