# Vendor Criticality and Vendor Risk Rating

The GRC Practice Lab provides both vendor criticality information and vendor risk assessment information.

These are separate attributes in the captured evidence.

For example:

- Amazon Web Services is classified as **Critical** in the vendor inventory and has a **90% Low** risk assessment.
- Stripe is classified as **Critical** in the vendor inventory and has an **86% Low** risk assessment.
- SendGrid is classified as **Medium** in the vendor inventory and has a **59% High** risk assessment.

The evidence therefore shows that vendor criticality and vendor risk rating are recorded separately in the GRC Practice Lab.

# Assessment and Evidence Scope

The vendor inventory contains eight vendors, while the captured Vendor Risk Assessment register contains five completed vendor assessments.

Therefore, the assessment results documented in this project apply to the five vendors shown in the captured assessment evidence.

The available evidence does not provide vendor risk assessment results for the remaining three vendors.

No additional vendor assessment or treatment information is inferred beyond the information displayed in the GRC Practice Lab.
