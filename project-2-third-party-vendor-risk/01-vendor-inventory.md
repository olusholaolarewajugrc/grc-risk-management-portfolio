# Vendor Inventory

## Objective

The objective of this phase is to document the organization's third-party vendor inventory, including the services provided, vendor criticality, operational status, and responsible business owner.

The vendor inventory was developed using the Vendor Management section of the GRC Practice Lab.

---

# Vendor Inventory Methodology

The vendor inventory was reviewed to identify:

1. Third-party vendors used by the organization.
2. Services provided by each vendor.
3. Vendor criticality classification.
4. Current vendor status.
5. Responsible business or security owner.

The inventory provides the foundation for subsequent vendor risk assessment and treatment activities.
