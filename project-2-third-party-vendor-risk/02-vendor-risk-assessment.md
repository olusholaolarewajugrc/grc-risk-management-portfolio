# Vendor Risk Assessment

## Objective

The objective of this phase is to assess third-party vendors across security, compliance, financial, and operational dimensions and document the resulting vendor risk ratings.

The vendor risk assessments were performed using the Vendor Risk Assessment section of the GRC Practice Lab.

---

# Vendor Risk Assessment Methodology

The GRC Practice Lab assesses vendors across four risk dimensions:

- **Security (SEC)**
- **Compliance (COMP)**
- **Financial (FIN)**
- **Operational (OPS)**

Each assessment dimension is scored out of 25 points.

The overall vendor assessment score is presented as a percentage and is associated with a vendor risk rating of Critical, High, Medium, or Low within the GRC Practice Lab.

The assessment register also records the date on which each vendor assessment was completed.

# Vendor Criticality and Risk Assessment

The vendor inventory and vendor risk assessment provide two different views of third-party risk.

Vendor criticality identifies the importance of the vendor to the organization, while the vendor risk assessment records the assessed risk posture based on the assessment dimensions shown in the GRC Practice Lab.

For example:

- Amazon Web Services is classified as **Critical** in the vendor inventory and has a **90% Low** risk assessment.
- Stripe is classified as **Critical** in the vendor inventory and has an **86% Low** risk assessment.
- SendGrid is classified as **Medium** in the vendor inventory and has a **59% High** risk assessment.

This demonstrates that vendor criticality and vendor risk rating are separate assessment attributes.
